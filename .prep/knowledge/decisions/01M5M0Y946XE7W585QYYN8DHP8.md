title: 'Curve SHPE: exponential fall at the low end, flat hold at the top'
description: 'SHPE 0-50 blends a normalized exponential fall into the half sine (m = shape*2, k = (1-m)*expCurve), giving a cusp at the peak for percussive envelopes; SHPE 50-100 is sine^p widening to a flat hold (p <= 0 returns 1). Steepness is the per-track Shape Curve setting: Gentle 4 / Medium 6 (default) / Snappy 8. Skew endpoints are exact, not clamped: skew 0 = instant attack, skew 100 = instant release. Why: every power of a sine has zero slope at the peak, so it can only make a rounded top.'
